QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FAQ.

Everything you need to know.

Is PlotTwisted anonymous?
Posts are designed to be shared without displaying the poster's name.
Can everyone see my confession?
Posts submitted to the public community feed can be viewed by other visitors.
Can I post a hugot?
Yes. Go to Hugot Corner, write your message, and post it.
How does Sign or Delusion work?
Read the situation and choose the verdict you believe fits best.
Is the roulette random?
Yes. Every spin chooses a random scenario from the PlotTwisted collection.
What can I write?
Keep posts respectful and avoid sharing private information about yourself or others.
