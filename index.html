ARCHIVE INFORMATION

FAQ.

Questions submitted to the archive keeper.

Is PlotTwisted anonymous?
Posts are designed to be shared without publicly displaying the poster's name.
Can everyone see my confession?
Posts submitted to the public community archive can be viewed by visitors.
Can I post a hugot?
Yes. Visit the Hugot Corner, write your entry, and submit it to the archive.
How does Sign or Delusion work?
Read the case file and choose the verdict that you think best fits the situation.
Is the roulette random?
Yes. The Fate Machine randomly selects an entry from the PlotTwisted collection.
Is the Compatibility Test real?
No. The Compatibility Test is a fictional entertainment feature. The percentage and fate are randomly generated for fun.
