Archive Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PlotTwisted?
PlotTwisted is a vintage-inspired archive for confessions, thoughts, messages, stories and playful interactive features.
Are the posts anonymous?
No public name is displayed with submitted posts. However, users should avoid sharing private or identifying information.
Can I submit a confession?
Yes. Open the Feelings collection and select Confessions.
What is Fate Roulette?
It is a playful randomizer that reveals one of the archive's fictional plot twists.
Is the compatibility result real?
No. The compatibility test is intended only as entertainment.
Can I remove a record?
If a record needs to be addressed, use the Contact section to communicate with the archive administrator.
About the Archive

PlotTwisted

PlotTwisted is a fictional Victorian-inspired archive designed to collect stories, confessions, unsent thoughts and playful plot twists.

The archive combines an old-letter aesthetic with interactive features, creating a space where ordinary thoughts can be presented like mysterious records waiting to be discovered.

Every record has a story. Every story has a twist.
