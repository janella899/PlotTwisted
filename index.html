QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FAQ.

Everything you need to know.

Is PlotTwisted anonymous?
Yes. Posts are designed to be shared without displaying the poster's name.
Can everyone see my confession?
Posts submitted to the public community feed can be viewed by other visitors.
Can I post a hugot?
Yes. Go to Hugot Corner, write your message, and post it.
How does Sign or Delusion work?
Read the situation and choose Sign, Maybe, or Delulu.
Is the roulette random?
Yes. The roulette chooses a random entry from the PlotTwisted collection.
What can I write?
Keep your posts respectful and avoid sharing private information.
