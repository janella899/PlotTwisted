QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FAQ.

Everything you need to know about PlotTwisted.

Is PlotTwisted anonymous?
Yes. Posts are designed to be shared without displaying the poster's name.
Can everyone see my confession?
Posts submitted to the public community feed can be viewed by other visitors.
Can I post a hugot?
Yes. Go to Hugot Corner, write your message, and press POST HUGOT.
How does Sign or Delusion work?
Read the situation and choose Sign, Maybe, or Delulu. The community results update based on submitted votes.
Is the roulette actually random?
Yes. The roulette chooses a random entry from the PlotTwisted plot-twist collection.
What can I write?
Keep your posts respectful and avoid sharing private information about yourself or other people.
