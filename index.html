About Us.

Why PlotTwisted?

PlotTwisted is an anonymous community space where people can share confessions, hugots, unsent messages, and unexpected stories without having to reveal their identity.


It is a place to express thoughts, discover different perspectives, have fun with plot twists, and connect through stories.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED

FAQ.

A few things you might want to know.

Are my posts anonymous?

PlotTwisted is designed for anonymous sharing. Avoid putting your real name, address, phone number, or other private information in a post.

Who can see my posts?

Posts marked as public can be displayed in the community feed for other visitors to see.

Can I post a confession?

Yes. Use the confession box in the Community section and submit your story.

What is Sign or Delusion?

It is a community voting feature where people can decide whether a situation looks like a sign, maybe, or simply delusion.

What should I avoid posting?

Do not post private information about yourself or someone else, threats, harassment, or content intended to harm another person.
